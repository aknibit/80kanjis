# Pronunciation audio

WORDS (232), SENTENCES (480), COUNTER SENTENCES (266), VERB SENTENCES (236) AND ADJECTIVE AND ADVERB SENTENCES (250) — AivisSpeech (morioki)
All the audio of the site is generated speech; there are no human recordings.
Files: words/*.mp3, one per word of the vocabulary (collection.json);
sentences/*.mp3, one per sentence of the kanji dialogs (content/kanji-sentences.json);
counters/*.mp3, one per sentence of the counter tables, their last column
(content/counters.json); verbs/*.mp3, one per sentence of the four forms of each
verb (content/verbs.json); and modifiers/*.mp3, one per example of the adjectives
and the adverbs (content/modifiers.json).
Credit: AivisSpeech: morioki.
Voice: morioki 1.0.0, style ノーマル (voice by もりおき, X @morioki_5; model by yuki,
X @ai_shirohana), from AivisHub:
https://hub.aivis-project.com/aivm-models/baaae3c0-7b22-4605-8ba5-80c959b41a48
Engine: AivisSpeech Engine 1.2.0, run locally (https://aivis-project.com/).
Encoded as MP3 (32 kbps, mono) with LAME.
License of the voice model: Aivis Common Model License (ACML) 1.0.
https://github.com/Aivis-Project/ACML/blob/master/ACML-1.0.md
It allows personal and commercial use, with optional credit, but not uses that
pass the audio off as the speaker's own or official, harm the speaker's image,
attack or deceive people, or campaign for political or religious causes.
The text, the spelling given to the engine, the spoken kana with accents, the
reading corrections and the SHA-256 of each file are in words/generation.json,
sentences/generation.json, counters/generation.json, verbs/generation.json and
modifiers/generation.json; scripts/generate-audio.py regenerates them.
These files have not received a native-speaker listening or pronunciation review.
