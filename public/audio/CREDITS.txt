The MP3 content is unmodified; filenames were shortened. These are twelve sample
recordings for evaluation, not a linguistically reviewed pronunciation curriculum.
The source describes a mix of a native Japanese male professional voice actor
(Tokyo accent) and a native Japanese female amateur voice actor (Kansai accent).
Individual speaker identity is not supplied in the file metadata.
