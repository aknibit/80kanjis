In particular, 中 has the relevant reading ジュウ, used in 一日中, in addition
to チュウ. It is included even though this KANJIDIC2 entry omits it.

Editorial conventions:
- On readings use katakana; kun readings use hiragana.
- A dot marks okurigana (み.る -> 見る); it is not pronounced.
- Basic readings are not mechanically assigned to pieces of irregular words.
- Every modal derives all matching vocabulary and full-word readings from
  collection.json, including occurrences in other rows and sound changes.
- Existing vocabulary definitions, audio, and difficulty ratings are retained.

Refresh procedure: regularly compare a current KANJIDIC2 release with this
reviewed selection; verify relevant differences against dictionary entries,
then update only the reviewed kanji fields and kanjiReview metadata in
collection.json. Recheck all vocabulary examples and run the project tests.
The saved version/date identify the dictionary snapshot used for this review.
